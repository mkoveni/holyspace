# Divine Community Space: Getting started

A practical guide for church staff and ministry leaders using the Church Management Portal.

Updated: 12 October 2026. This guide describes the current screens. Your account may show only the areas you have permission to use.

## Quick start: your first day

1. Get the website address, username, and password from your administrator.
2. Sign in and open **People**. Search for someone you already know to become familiar with a person's profile.
3. Check **Families** to see how people are grouped into households.
4. Open **Events** to view church gatherings, or **Life Events** to view personal milestones.
5. Use the section of this guide that matches your work. You do not need to set up every area yourself.
6. When finished, open your account menu at the top right and select **Sign out**.

If you are setting up the church's information for the first time, start with People. Many later tasks require you to select people who have already been added.

## Find your way around

Use the menu on the left to move between areas. On a smaller screen, use the menu button at the top to show or hide it.

| Area | What you use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | See a community summary and recently added people and life events. |
| People | Keep personal details, contact information, membership status, and relationships. |
| Families | Group people into households and choose a main contact. |
| Life Events | Record births, marriages, engagements, baptisms, and other personal milestones. |
| Events | Schedule services, meetings, classes, and outreach gatherings. |
| Volunteer workspace | Review your own serving schedule and respond to invitations. |
| Service planning | Prepare and publish ordered service items and resource links. |
| Volunteering | Approve volunteers and assign them to shifts. |
| Children's Ministry | Enrol children, prepare staffed sessions, and record arrival and collection. |
| Giving | Record donations, pledges, acknowledgements, and reconciliation. |
| Attendance | Record participation and visitor numbers. |
| Group workspace | View your groups, their meetings, and roster attendance. |
| Ministries | Manage ministry teams, small groups, members, and leaders. |
| Forms | Publish sign-up/contact forms and review visitor responses. |
| Communications | Prepare email or SMS campaigns and review delivery progress. |
| User Accounts, Roles, Permissions | Administrator tools for login accounts and access. |
| Settings | View information about integrations planned for future setup screens. |

**Events** and **Life Events** have different purposes. A Sunday service belongs in Events. A person's marriage belongs in Life Events.

### Common buttons and forms

- **Add** or **Create** opens a form for a new record.
- **Save** stores your changes. Wait for confirmation before moving on.
- **Cancel** inside a form closes it without saving. A **Cancel** action beside an existing record can change that record's status; read the action carefully.
- **Details** or the eye icon opens information about a record.
- **Edit** or the pencil icon opens a record for changes.
- **Retry** tries to load information again after an error.

Volunteering, Children's Ministry, Giving, Attendance, Ministries, and Communications use tabs across the top. Choose a tab, then use its **Add**, **Details**, or other available actions. On Attendance and Giving, select your filters and click **Apply filters**.

When a form asks you to select a person, type their name, email, or phone in the search field. Check the contact details shown beneath the name to choose the right person. Fields such as campaign audiences allow you to select more than one person.

Some fields cannot be changed after creation. For example, an attendance correction changes the status, but keeps the original person and event occurrence.

### Dates and times

An **occurrence** is one dated gathering within an event. “Sunday Service” is the event; “Sunday Service on 18 October at 09:00” is an occurrence.

When creating an event, enter its times in the **Timezone** selected in the form. Other operational forms, such as volunteer availability and shifts, use your browser's timezone, shown above the form. Check your computer's timezone before entering times.

## People: add and maintain a person's information

### Add a person

1. Open **People** and select the add button.
2. Enter the person's first and last name.
3. Add the date of birth, gender, email, and phone where known.
4. Save, then open the person's name to view their profile.

Search before adding someone to avoid duplicate records. Enter an accurate date of birth for children; age checks depend on it.

### Update a profile

Open the person and select **Edit** to change their basic details. Use **Membership status** to update their status.

The profile also has tabs for **Addresses**, **Communication**, **Education**, and **Employment**. Open the relevant tab and use its add or edit controls.

For messaging, keep the main email and phone in the person's basic details up to date. Contact details and permission to send messages are separate: campaign consent is recorded in Communications.

### Add a relationship

1. Make sure both people exist in People.
2. Open the first person's profile.
3. In **Relationships**, select **Add**.
4. Choose the relationship type and related person, then save.
5. Open the second person's profile to check the corresponding relationship.

Relationships appear from both people's profiles, with the corresponding direction where appropriate. Use the end action when a relationship is no longer current.

## Families: organise households

1. Open **Families** and select **Add family**.
2. Enter the family name and save.
3. Open the family and add its members, selecting each person's role in the family.
4. Add the household address where available.
5. Use the primary-contact control beside a member to choose the main contact.

A primary contact is the person normally contacted about the household. Family membership does not automatically authorise someone to collect a child from Children's Ministry.

The family overview also shows upcoming personal milestones, including annual celebrations from recorded life events. If a celebration is missing, check the Life Events record, its date, and its participants.

## Life Events: milestones, birthdays, and anniversaries

### Record a milestone

1. Open **Life Events** and select **Record Event**.
2. Choose the event type, such as birth, marriage, engagement, or baptism.
3. Enter the original event date and time.
4. Select the people involved and each participant's role.
5. Add any helpful notes and select **Record Event**.

For a marriage, select both spouses and use the spouse role. For a birth, select the person whose birth is being recorded with the appropriate role.

Open a milestone to review or edit it. Its detail screen also offers status and delete actions. Check that you have selected the correct record before using them.

### Find birthdays or anniversaries in a month

1. Open **Life Events**.
2. Choose **Birth** for birthdays or **Marriage** for wedding anniversaries in **Filter by type**.
3. Select a **Month** and **Year**.
4. Turn on **Annual recurrences**.

For example, Marriage + October + 2026 + Annual recurrences includes marriages recorded in October of earlier years, up to 2026. The table still shows each original event date.

With Annual recurrences switched off, month and year filters find the original events in that period. You can also filter other milestone types, including engagements.

Record the original milestone once; you do not need a new copy every year. This screen searches recorded Life Events, so a date of birth entered only on a person's profile is not a substitute for a Birth life-event record here.

## Events: plan church gatherings

### Create a gathering

1. Open **Events** and select **Create event**.
2. Enter the title, event type, description, and location.
3. Choose the timezone and enter the start and end times.
4. Choose **One-off** or **Weekly**.
5. For a weekly event, choose the number of occurrences, from 1 to 52.
6. Create the event and review its occurrences.

Example: create Sunday Service at 09:00–10:30 in Africa/Johannesburg, with a weekly schedule for eight occurrences.

### Change one gathering

Open the event, find the occurrence, and use its edit control. Change its times, location, or status, then select **Save occurrence**. Choose cancelled to cancel that occurrence, or scheduled to restore it.

Editing the event's title or description is separate from changing an occurrence's schedule.

After rescheduling, review linked volunteer assignments and children's sessions. They may need confirmation again. Restoring a cancelled occurrence does not automatically restore every linked commitment.

## Volunteering: build a rota

Set up volunteers in this order:

1. **Roles:** add the jobs needed, such as Welcome Team or Children's Teacher. Record screening and qualification requirements.
2. **Volunteer profiles:** select an existing person, approve their roles, enter availability windows, and record applicable screening and qualification expiry dates. Keep **Active volunteer** on for current volunteers.
3. **Shifts:** choose the event occurrence and role, enter start and end times, and set the number of positions.
4. **Assignments:** select the shift and volunteer, then save.

Availability must cover the whole shift. The system also checks approval, required qualifications, screening, overlapping assignments, and available positions.

Use **Confirm**, **Decline**, or **Cancel** on assignments as appropriate. A status of **needs_review** means you should review the changed arrangements and confirm eligibility again. Review the shift first, then confirm assignments.

For Children's Ministry staffing, each staff member needs a confirmed, eligible assignment in a screening-required role for the same event occurrence. The shift can cover part of the occurrence; it does not need to span the entire event. Screening, required qualifications and availability must cover the staff member's full shift.

## Children's Ministry: enrolment, arrival, and collection

For assigned staff, use **Children’s station** to find enrolled children, print paired labels and verify collection. See [the step-by-step station guide](children-check-in-stations.md) for account setup, lost-label replacement and private support notes.


### Prepare before the first session

1. Add the children and collecting adults to **People**, including accurate birth dates for children.
2. Prepare the event occurrence in **Events**.
3. Set up screened volunteers and their assignments in **Volunteering**.
4. In **Children's Ministry → Programmes**, add the programme, such as Sunday School.
5. In **Classes**, select the programme and enter the class name, age range, and capacity. Classes are for children under 18.
6. In **Enrolments**, select the class, child, and enrolment date.
7. In **Pickup authorisations**, select the child, authorised adult, and expiry date.
8. In **Sessions**, select the class, event occurrence, and eligible staff.

Only record screening and pickup authorisation after following your church's approval process. The software records those decisions; it does not perform screening or verify an adult's identity.

### Check a child in

1. Open **Check-ins** and use the add button.
2. Choose the session, child, and receiving staff member.
3. Save and check that the open check-in appears.

Check-in opens 30 minutes before the occurrence starts and remains available until it ends. The child needs active enrolment, a suitable age, and an available place. The session needs eligible staff. A child cannot have two open check-ins at once.

### Record collection

1. Open **Collection** and use the add button.
2. Select the child's open check-in.
3. Verify the collecting adult's identity using your church's handover procedure.
4. Select the adult and supervising session staff member, then save.
5. Check that the check-in now has a checkout time.

The adult must have a current, explicit pickup authorisation. Being a parent or family contact alone is not enough. Use **Revoke** when an authorisation should stop being valid.

Cancelling a session does not check children out. Continue recording each handover for children already in your care. After rescheduling an event, review staffing and edit the session to confirm it again before accepting new arrivals.

## Giving: donations and records

### Record a donation

1. Add the available **Funds**, such as General Fund or Outreach.
2. Open **Donations** and use the add button.
3. Enter the amount in normal currency units: for R150, enter **150.00**, not 15000.
4. Enter the currency code, such as **ZAR**.
5. Add the fund allocations. Their total must equal the donation amount.
6. Enter the received date and method: cash, bank, or other.
7. Select the donor, or leave it blank for an anonymous donation. Add an event occurrence or source reference if useful.
8. Save and review the record.

### Acknowledge a donation

Use **Issue acknowledgement** beside a posted donation. Then use **View and print an acknowledgement** to select it and choose **Open acknowledgement**. Print it from the acknowledgement screen.

An acknowledgement records the gift; it should not be treated as confirmation of tax deductibility.

### Correct, reconcile, and report

- **Reversals:** select the donation, reversal date, and reason. A reversal records the full reversal and voids a linked acknowledgement. It does not send money back through a bank or payment provider. Record a replacement donation separately if needed.
- **Pledges:** record a donor's promise, fund, amount, currency, and due date. Use **Mark fulfilled** or **Cancel** when appropriate. Recording a donation does not automatically fulfil a pledge.
- **Reconciliation batches:** select donations, enter the expected total, currency, statement reference, and reconciliation date. The selected donations must balance and must not already belong to another batch.
- **Fund totals:** select the date range and optional fund, then choose **Apply filters**. Review gross donations, reversals, and net totals by currency. Reversals use their reversal dates in period totals.

## Attendance: record participation

1. Open **Attendance → Attendance records** and use the add button.
2. Select the occurrence and person.
3. Choose present, absent, or excused, then save.
4. In **Visitor counts**, record anonymous visitors who have not been entered as named attendees.
5. Use the occurrence or person filters and **Apply filters** to review records and totals.

Children's check-in attendance appears in **Children's attendance**. The totals avoid counting the same named person twice across manual and children's attendance. Do not include named attendees again in anonymous visitor counts.

To correct a record or visitor count, choose **Edit**, make the change, and enter a reason. Corrections preserve history. Children's check-in presence cannot be contradicted by marking the same child absent or excused.

## Ministries: teams and small groups

1. In **Ministries and groups**, add a name, choose Ministry or Small group, and add a description.
2. In **Memberships**, choose the group, person, joined date, and Member or Leader role.
3. Use **Make leader** or **Make member** when responsibilities change.
4. Use **End membership** when someone leaves; this records the current local date.
5. Use **Archive** when the ministry or group is no longer active.

Archiving preserves its records and prevents new members from joining it. A ministry leader role describes responsibility within that ministry; it does not grant login permissions.

## Communications: send church updates

Before your first campaign, ask your administrator to confirm that the intended email or SMS provider and background delivery service are ready. The Settings page does not currently provide provider configuration controls.

### Prepare and queue a campaign

1. Check the recipients' main email or phone details in **People**.
2. In **Contact consent**, record each person's consent for the intended channel and the supporting note. Email and SMS consent are separate.
3. In **Templates**, add a name, channel, message, and an email subject where applicable. Use **{{name}}** to insert the recipient's name.
4. In **Campaigns**, select the template and audience, then save the draft.
5. Review the campaign's **Details**, including the message and recipients.
6. When ready to send, select **Queue for delivery**.
7. Review **Delivery history** for progress.

A campaign keeps a copy of the template content selected when it is created. Create a new campaign if you need revised content. Queuing enables delivery by the background service; it does not mean every message has already been sent.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| pending | Waiting for a delivery attempt. |
| sending | An attempt has started; its final outcome may still need checking. |
| delivered | The sending transport accepted the message; this does not prove the recipient read or received it. |
| failed | A failure was recorded. |
| suppressed | The message was withheld, for example because consent or contact details were missing. |

Use **Withdraw consent** when a person no longer agrees to that channel. Cancelling a campaign cannot recall messages already sent.

If a delivery stays pending, check **Provider status** and contact your administrator. For uncertain sending or failed outcomes, an authorised operator can verify the provider's records, use **Edit** in Delivery history, and record the verified outcome and evidence. Do not guess or create another campaign to retry an uncertain message. A sending record must be at least five minutes old before it can be resolved.

## Accounts and access: for administrators

A People profile and a login account are separate. Adding a person does not give them access to the portal.

Administrators with account-management access can:

- Create a **User Account**, with a temporary password of at least 12 characters.
- Assign a role through the account's management dialog by choosing its **Role** from the catalogue.
- Attach existing permissions to roles using **Manage Permissions** and the **Permission** selector.
- Review effective access with **Refresh Permissions** in the account dialog.
- Update passwords, disable accounts, or enable them in **Security**.

Roles and Permissions load the existing church catalogues, and assignment dialogs provide selectors. User Accounts still supports **Look up by ID** and keeps accounts opened or created during the session; its table is not a complete account directory. Keep account IDs available for managing existing accounts.

Ask users to sign out and sign in again after access changes so the menu reflects their current permissions. If you are a new user who cannot sign in or see a required area, ask your administrator rather than creating a second account.

## Review changes and solve common problems

In the operational areas, choose **Details** to see **Change history**, showing recorded creation and updates, the account, and the time. Use **Earlier changes** for older entries. History starts when the audit journal was enabled; earlier changes are not reconstructed.

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A menu item is missing or access is denied | Ask your administrator to check your permissions and account status. |
| A person is missing from a selection list | Check that they exist in People, then reload the page. |
| A save says the record changed | Copy any unsaved notes, reload, review the latest record, and apply your changes again. |
| A volunteer cannot be assigned | Check approved roles, active status, availability, screening, qualifications, overlapping shifts, and vacancies. |
| A child's check-in is rejected | Check the time window, enrolment, age, capacity, session status, eligible staff, and other open check-ins. |
| Collection is rejected | Check the adult's authorisation and expiry, and select a supervising session staff member. |
| A donation will not save | Check the amount, currency, allocation total, and whether its source reference was already used. |
| An anniversary is missing | Check the original Life Event, participants, type, month, year, and Annual recurrences switch. |
| A page will not load | Use Retry. If it persists, contact your administrator with the page and error message. |

After an uncertain save, check whether the record exists before adding it again. For support, note what you were trying to do and the exact error; never include passwords or provider credentials.

## A simple weekly routine

Before a gathering, review the occurrence, volunteer rota, children's staffing, enrolments, and pickup authorisations. During the gathering, record attendance and children's arrivals and handovers. Afterwards, check that no child remains incorrectly marked as checked in, record donations, reconcile giving, and review any outstanding delivery problems.

Keep People and Families up to date as changes arise. Use Life Events each month to prepare birthday and anniversary lists.


## Register people for an event

1. Open **Events**, choose the event, and scroll to **Event registration**.
2. Choose the occurrence you want to manage. Each date has its own registration list and settings.
3. Set **Registration capacity**, or leave it blank for unlimited places. Turn on **Registrations open**, choose whether to enable the waitlist, and click **Save registration settings**.
4. Find a person using **Attendee**, select the match, and click **Register attendee**. Add new people through People first.
5. Check the roster: **confirmed** means a place is reserved; **waitlisted** means the person is waiting for a place.
6. To cancel, use **Cancel registration**, enter a reason, and confirm. The next waiting person automatically receives a freed place. Expand the row using its arrow to see change history.

You need event management permission to make changes and People read permission to search. Read-only staff can view the roster. If another staff member changes a record first, reload and review the current state before retrying. Registration settings start closed, and cancelled or ended occurrences cannot accept people.

Turning off new registrations or the waitlist preserves people already waiting. Increasing capacity can promote them. A person who rejoins after cancellation goes to the end of the queue. Use **Next page** and **First page** to navigate long rosters.

Registration reserves a place; record actual participation in Attendance. It does not send confirmation messages or collect payments. Review reservations when you reschedule or restore an occurrence, and inform attendees of any changes.


## Publish a form and review responses

Open **Forms**, choose **Create form**, and add a title, introduction and fields. Save the draft and click **Publish**. Use **Open public form** to check it, then choose **Review responses** to copy the shareable link. Visitors do not need a staff account.

New responses wait for staff review. Open each one, check the answers, select approve/reject, and record a note. Search existing people before linking; create a new person explicitly only after checking their identity. For an event form, approval also creates a confirmed or waitlisted registration. A public submission alone does not reserve a place.

Unpublish before editing the form or to close submissions. Old responses keep their original wording. Read-only Forms access does not expose visitor responses. See [the forms guide](forms.md) for permission and setup details.


## Giving statements and pledge progress

Open **Giving → Statements and reports**. Choose a date range and optionally find a donor or choose a fund, then click **Generate report**. Currency totals stay separate. Use **Print statement/report** or **Download CSV** after reviewing the entries. A reversal appears in the period when it was recorded, so a period can have negative net giving.

Under **Pledge progress**, choose a date and refresh. To credit a pledge, select it, select a compatible posted gift, enter the amount and click **Apply gift to pledge**. The system checks that the money has not already been assigned beyond the gift/fund or pledge amount. Correct a mistake with **Cancel assignment** and a reason; history is retained. Pledge status does not change automatically.

See [Giving statements and reports](giving-reports.md) for the detailed rules.


## Group meetings and leader attendance

Managers create groups and memberships in **Ministries**, schedule gatherings in **Events**, and link an occurrence in **Group workspace**. Use separate occurrences for different groups.

Leaders need a login account linked to their Person record, an active leader membership, and `ministries.lead` permission. Open **Group workspace**, choose your group and meeting, then record present, absent or excused for roster members. Corrections require a reason and preserve history. Leaders do not need church-wide People or Attendance access.

Cancelling a meeting closes new attendance but retains existing entries. Removing leadership or archiving a group revokes leader access. See [the group workspace guide](group-workspace.md) for setup and detailed rules.


## Volunteer invitations and service orders

Coordinators prepare shifts in Volunteering, then open **Volunteer invitations** to invite an eligible person and set a deadline. Pending invitations reserve places but are not confirmed children's staffing. Volunteers need an account linked to their Person record and `volunteering.serve` permission. In **Volunteer workspace**, they can accept or decline their own pending invitations and view related published service orders.

Use **Service planning** to create an ordered draft, add item durations and resource links, then publish it after review. Editing or rescheduling requires review/publication again. **Prepare email reminder** creates a draft campaign only; review and queue it in Communications. Consent, current eligibility and current shift/invitation state are checked before delivery.

See [Volunteer responses and service planning](volunteer-responses-and-service-planning.md) for setup and detailed rules.

## Member accounts

Members use **My church** after enabling an authenticator. They can update their own contact details, view household membership, cancel their registrations, and print personal giving statements. See [the member portal guide](member-portal.md) for access setup, MFA and account recovery.

## Reports and follow-up work

Open **Reports and segments** to run permitted operational reports, export CSVs, and save People filters. Assign follow-ups from a segment result and record progress in **Follow-up tasks**. Daily automation is opt-in and creates tasks only. See [the reporting and follow-up guide](reporting-segments-and-followups.md).

## Online giving and paid events

Members open **My church → Giving and tickets**. Staff open **Giving → Online payments** to enable funds and review payment/refund records, and configure ticket prices in the event registration section. Payfast and Yoco handle hosted ZAR checkout; Payfast also handles monthly giving. See [the online payments guide](online-payments.md) for setup and operating instructions.

## Adding pictures

Open a saved person, family or event and use its picture panel. For a public form, open **Forms**, choose **Edit** on the saved form, and use **Form banner**. Choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP file of at most 5 MB, review its preview, then click **Upload image**. You can replace or remove it later. Changing images requires management access for that part of the system. Public form banners appear on the shared page while the form is published. See [Portal photos and banners](portal-images.md) for the full instructions.
